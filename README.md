# Classmate AI 🎓

**Not an AI tutor. A confused classmate who
needs your help.*

The best way to learn is to teach. Classmate AI flips the traditional AI study tool on its head -- instead of AI giving you answers, **you teach a confused AI classmate**

## How It Works

1. **Choose a subject** -- Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths, and more
2. **Set the difficulty** -- How confused is your classmate?
3. **Teach them** -- Explain concepts, correct their mistakes, answer their questions
4. **Get evaluated** -- A silent AI analyzes your understanding and finds your weak spots

## Key Features

- 👍 **Confused Classmate AI** -- The AI acts like a real student who needs help, not a teacher
- 💾 *Intentional Mistakes** -- The AI makes plausible errors for you to catch and correct
- 🌏 *Copy-Paste Detection** -- Can't cheat by pasting textbook answers
- 📊 **Silent Evaluator** -- A second AI watches the conversation and analyzes your understanding
- 🐍 *Weak Spot Detection** -- Finds exactly what you're struggling with
- �
 *Session Reports** -- Detailed breakdown of what you understand vs what needs work

## Tech Stack

- Frontend: Next.js 14, React 18, Tailwind CSS - Backend: Next.js API Routes - AI: OpenAI API (GPT-4o-mini) - Architecture: Dual-AI (Classmate + Evaluator)

## The Philosophy
> Don't ask AI to do your thinking. Teach AI to prove that you understand.
Most AI study tools are passive -- you ask, AI answers. Classmate AI forces active recall and the Feynman Technique.
